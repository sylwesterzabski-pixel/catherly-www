## Ścieżka 1 — pracujesz sama (id="pracujesz-sama")

### H2

You still keep it all in your head and in your messages.

### CO BOLI

Every morning you decide from scratch where to start: your notes are on your phone, what you agreed is in your messages, and your conversation dates sit in several places at once. In the evening, you’re not sure whether you’ve forgotten someone important.

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 1

You set the order of your conversations in the Daily Action Plan — you start the day with something concrete instead of hesitating. You put your conversation dates into the calendar with reminders — 30 minutes before each conversation Catherly reminds you about it.

**Linki:** „Daily Action Plan" → /funkcje/pozyskiwanie#dmo ·
„calendar with reminders" → /funkcje/pozyskiwanie#kalendarz

*(„30 minutes" wyłącznie komponentem liczby z content/facts.json —
przypomnienie-kalendarza-minuty; literał w JSX nie przejdzie lintera)*

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 2

When someone asks you about working together, you pass on the link to your sign-up form — the completed submission goes straight into your contact base. You give a hard conversation a dry run in the Training Room first.

**Linki:** „sign-up form" → /funkcje/pozyskiwanie#formularz ·
„Training Room" → /funkcje/pozyskiwanie#sala-treningowa

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 3

You start a post from a ready template, not from a blank page. The Shield points out risky wording before you publish.

**Linki:** „ready template" → /funkcje/tresci#szablony ·
„Shield" → /funkcje/tresci#tarcza

### OD KTÓREGO PLANU

Everything above works from the Starter plan up.

**Link:** See pricing → /cennik

### CZEGO TA ŚCIEŻKA NIE ZAŁATWIA

Catherly won’t find contacts for you and won’t lead your conversations — you send people to the form yourself, and what you get in return is order in your day.

## Ścieżka 2 — budujesz zespół (id="budujesz-zespol")

### H2

You have a team. The question is who to help today and who not to disturb.

### CO BOLI

Everyone on your team is in a different place: one is only just settling in, another has slowed down after a good start. You don’t have it on one screen, so you find out in conversation — usually later than you’d like.

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 1

The Dashboard shows where things stand right now: sales, active contacts, your team’s activity — all in one place. You bring a new person in through the onboarding wizard: profile, goals, materials, invitation.

**Linki:** „Dashboard" → /funkcje/wyniki#pulpit ·
„onboarding wizard" → /funkcje/zespol#kreator-wdrozeniowy

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 2

Then you guide her through First 90 Days: missions tell her what to do now, and four phases show her where she’s heading. Achievements run on ready-made rules: your team collects badges for the steps that follow, and when a colleague comes back after a break, a grace token protects her streak.

**Linki:** „First 90 Days" → /funkcje/zespol#pierwsze-90-dni ·
„Achievements" → /funkcje/zespol#osiagniecia

### OD KTÓREGO PLANU

Everything above works from the Starter plan up. On the Growth plan, you see the signs someone may be stepping back, with a ready line to open the conversation.

**Link:** See pricing → /cennik

### CZEGO TA ŚCIEŻKA NIE ZAŁATWIA

Catherly won’t decide for you who has slowed down — you see only what you and your team record in the app, and it’s you who leads the conversation.

## Ścieżka 3 — prowadzisz strukturę (id="prowadzisz-strukture")

### H2

The structure has grown, and the decisions still come through you.

### CO BOLI

Questions from new people, content waiting to hear what you think, the uncertainty about what you’re allowed to write in which market — every one of them is waiting for you. The week doesn’t get any longer.

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 1

Team content approval sits with you: you approve a post or reject it, and the whole structure sees the status change right away. The Compliance Passport checks your message against four jurisdictions’ rules before you send it — you don’t have to keep the differences between markets in your head.

**Linki:** „Team content approval" →
/funkcje/zespol#zatwierdzanie-zespolu ·
„Compliance Passport" → /funkcje/zespol#paszport-zgodnosci

### CO CATHERLY Z TYM ROBI — akapit 2

In the Academy, the next module unlocks only once the previous one is finished — a new person doesn’t ask you where to start, and an administrator adds the training content.

**Linki:** „Academy" → /funkcje/zespol#akademia

### OD KTÓREGO PLANU

Everything above works from the Starter plan up. On the Growth plan, you see the full tree of your team structure. Clean export — your materials without a referral signature — is on the Pro plan.

**Link:** See pricing → /cennik

### CZEGO TA ŚCIEŻKA NIE ZAŁATWIA

The Compliance Passport gives no legal guarantee and no legal advice — it checks your message against the rules, and the approvals and the conversations with people stay with you.
