## Moduł 1 — Dashboard

### PO CO TO

On the Dashboard you see where things stand right now: sales, active contacts, your team’s activity — all in one place. Before you decide what matters most today, you know where you stand.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The Dashboard doesn’t show data from outside Catherly — you see what you and your team record in the app.

## Moduł 2 — Your Wrapped

### PO CO TO

You open Your Wrapped and read a summary of your period: what you did, what you sold, what was going on in your team. You see what actually happened — not just what you remember.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t turn your summary into a PDF report — you view Your Wrapped in the app.

## Moduł 3 — Goal with milestones

### PO CO TO

You set a goal and track your progress milestone by milestone. Instead of one distant finish line, you see how far you’ve come and what’s next.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly doesn’t set goals for you and doesn’t push you when progress slows — the goal is yours, and so is the pace.

## Moduł 4 — Success wall

### PO CO TO

A new person’s first sale, the milestone you closed — you save moments like these on the Success wall. You record and celebrate your successes and your team’s before everyday life buries them.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t record a success for you — what lands on the Success wall, you put there yourself.

## Moduł 5 — Testimony

### PO CO TO

You want to show your results to someone who hasn’t seen your day-to-day work. You export a CSV from the Testimony whenever you like — and the log with its SHA-256 digital fingerprint means your activity history can be verified, not just read.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The Testimony sends nothing anywhere — you see the log, and the CSV goes only to the people you yourself hand it to.

## Moduł 6 — Wall of Proof

### PO CO TO

You collect proof of your work on the Wall of Proof — your personal timeline of successes. On a harder day, you scroll through it and see how much is already behind you — it’s a space where you celebrate, not just measure.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The Wall of Proof isn’t the team’s wall — celebrating together has its place on the Success wall.
