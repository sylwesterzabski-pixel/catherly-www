## Moduł 1 — Onboarding wizard

### PO CO TO

A friend has decided to join your team, and your day is already full of your own conversations. You don’t sit down with her to go over every setting — the onboarding wizard walks her through six steps, from her profile and goals to a ready start.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The wizard doesn’t move data over from other tools — the new person enters her profile and goals in it herself, step by step.

## Moduł 2 — Team content approval

### PO CO TO

Someone on your team has finished a post and is waiting to hear what you think. You approve it or reject it — and the whole structure sees the status change right away.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t publish an approved post for you or for anyone on your team — approval changes the status, and the author publishes the content herself.

## Moduł 3 — First 90 Days

### PO CO TO

A new person asks you what’s next — and you don’t build her plan from scratch. You guide her through First 90 Days: missions tell her what to do now, and four phases show her where she’s heading.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t guide the new person for you — the program gives her missions and phases, and it’s you who leads the conversations.

## Moduł 4 — Achievements

### PO CO TO

A colleague on your team comes back after a break, and her streak doesn’t start over from zero — a grace token protects it. For the steps that follow, she collects badges and sees that her daily work counts.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t let you design your own badges or the rules for earning them — your team collects achievements according to ready-made rules.

## Moduł 5 — Compliance Passport

### PO CO TO

What you’re allowed to write in one market can be off-limits in another — and you don’t have to keep those differences in your head. You check your message against four jurisdictions’ rules in the Compliance Passport before you send it.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The Compliance Passport gives no legal guarantee and no legal advice — the scanner checks your message against the rules, and you decide for yourself what you send.

## Moduł 6 — Academy

### PO CO TO

A new person on your team opens the Academy and doesn’t ask you where to start — the next module unlocks only once the previous one is finished. You get back to your own conversations, and her path takes care of itself.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly doesn’t come with ready-made training content — the Academy looks after the order of the modules, not what’s inside them.
