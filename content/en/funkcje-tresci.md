## Moduł 1 — Studio

### PO CO TO

The same idea has to look good in a post and in print — and you don’t want to start from scratch every time. That’s what the Studio is for: safe zones and auto-layout make sure nothing important gets cut off in any format.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

You can’t upload photos from your phone to the Studio — you put a design together from text, templates, and the editor’s elements.

## Moduł 2 — Templates with versioning

### PO CO TO

In the evening, you prepare a post, and instead of a blank page you open a ready template. You swap in your text, save your own version — and next time you start from that one.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t generate a template for you — you pick a ready one and adjust it your way.

## Moduł 3 — Hashtag sets

### PO CO TO

You put a hashtag set together once and have it at hand for every post — you add it with one click. You don’t retype anything from old posts or from the notes on your phone.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t suggest hashtags for you — you decide what’s in a set and which post it goes with.

## Moduł 4 — Publishing calendar

### PO CO TO

Every post idea gets its place and date in the calendar right away. You see what you’ve planned and for when, and when plans change, you move a post to another day — without rebuilding everything from scratch.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t publish a post for you — the calendar shows the plan, and you do the publishing yourself.

## Moduł 5 — Approval from your leader

### PO CO TO

You send a finished post for approval — from that moment it’s with your leader. When she accepts or rejects it, you see it right away — no chasing her in messages.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t email you about your leader’s decision — you see the status in the app.

## Moduł 6 — Shield

### PO CO TO

You catch risky wording before you publish, not after. The Shield’s rules run inside the app itself — your content never leaves it to be checked.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The Shield won’t fix the text for you — it points out risky wording, and what you do with it is your decision.

## Moduł 7 — Ethical Seal

### PO CO TO

You finish a design, and it gets the Ethical Seal — an ethical score with a public certificate. At a meeting you show your customer the QR code: she scans it and sees the certificate for herself.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t send the certificate to your customer — who sees it and when is your decision.

## Moduł 8 — Voice profile learning

### PO CO TO

You write posts the way you talk to your customers — in your own way. Catherly learns that style from your published posts and builds your brand’s voice profile from it.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

Catherly won’t write your posts for you — the voice profile remembers your style, and you do the writing yourself, in your own words.

## Moduł 9 — Post board with filters

### PO CO TO

You come back to your posts after a few days and don’t have to guess where you left off. You filter the board and see right away what’s waiting for approval and what you’ve already scheduled.

### CZEGO NIE ROBI

The board won’t show reach or likes — you see the status of your posts, and you check their results where you published them.

## Sekcja kierunku — asystent AI

### Treść

With the AI assistant, you write and edit content — it suggests, you decide.

### Granica

The assistant won’t write the content for you — it offers ideas and edits, and you write.
